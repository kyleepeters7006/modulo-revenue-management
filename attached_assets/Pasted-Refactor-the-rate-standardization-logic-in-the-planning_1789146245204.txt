Refactor the rate standardization logic in the planning model. The current implementation requires a room to qualify in EVERY month of the comparison window. That fixed the divisor churn but collapsed coverage (AL 56%, AL/MC 49%, SL 63%, VIL 66%, HC 27%), so the surviving cohort is now a biased sample rather than a representative one. Replace it with a chain-linked matched-pair index. 1. CHAIN-LINKED INDEX - Compute growth between ADJACENT periods only, on the set of rooms that qualify in both periods (call this the matched set M_t). Do not require full-window survival. - Link ratio for period t: sum(w_i * rate_i,t) / sum(w_i * rate_i,t-1), summed over i in M_t. - Quarterly growth = product of the 3 monthly links. YoY = product of 12 monthly links. - Re-base the chain annually (at the plan base period) so drift does not accumulate indefinitely. Store the base period explicitly. 2. FREEZE ELIGIBILITY AT THE BASE PERIOD - The outlier gate, base-rate exclusions, and payer filters must be adjudicated ONCE at the base period and then held constant for the life of that comparison. - A room that qualifies at base stays in the comparison at its actual historical rates even if it would fail the gate in a later month. - Do not re-run eligibility per month. Re-adjudicating monthly makes the qualifying rule itself a time series and manufactures rate movement. 3. FIXED (LASPEYRES) WEIGHTS - Weight by base-period room count or base-period revenue. Do not use current-period weights. - Current-period weighting reintroduces mix through the back door even with a clean matched cohort. 4. SAME-UNIT YoY PAIRING - For year-over-year, pair each room to ITSELF 12 months prior. Do not inherit a prior-year quarterly series that was built on a different qualifying set. - Current-period and prior-year-period must rest on the same cohort and the same weights. 5. RATE / MIX DECOMPOSITION (new output, do not suppress mix) - Decompose the raw period-over-period change into: a) matched-cohort rate effect (the chained index above) b) residual mix / composition effect (raw change minus rate effect) - Expose both in the plan output and in the API response. Mix is a real economic outcome, not noise, and must be reportable separately. 6. COVERAGE THRESHOLDS - MOVE FROM WINDOW TO LINK - Compute and store match coverage for EACH monthly link (share of currently priced rooms present in both periods), not for the window as a whole. - Floor is 90% per link. - If any link falls below 90%: SUPPRESS the derived growth number entirely (return null with a reason code) rather than displaying it with a caveat. Identify the specific failing month(s) in the reason code. - Remove the existing 70% full-window warning. 7. PARALLEL RUN - Keep the existing balanced-panel calculation available behind a flag or as a secondary field for the next two quarters so the two series can be compared. The gap between them is the entry/exit effect and needs to be inspectable before the old series is retired. 8. UNCHANGED - Level remains anchored to the full planning average. Only the price RELATIONSHIP comes from the matched cohort. Do not change the level anchor. - Cache invalidation behavior stays as-is; all scopes recalculate on next open.
