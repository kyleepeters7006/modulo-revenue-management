Please update Modulo’s pricing architecture so that the two existing user-facing rate concepts — “Modulo Rate” and “AI Rate” — are replaced with two clearer pricing strategy options:

1. Rules Rate
2. Revenue Target AI Rate

The goal is to simplify the product language and user experience while preserving the advanced AI Rate formula that already exists.

Important framing:
The current platform has two algorithm concepts called “Modulo Rate” and “AI Rate.” These are confusing to users because it is not clear when each should be used or how they are different.

Replace that structure with two clearly named pricing strategies:

- Rules Rate = transparent, stackable business rules pricing engine
- Revenue Target AI Rate = advanced AI-driven revenue optimization engine that utilizes the existing AI Rate formula

Do not continue presenting “Modulo Rate” and “AI Rate” as the two main user-facing algorithms. Treat those as legacy/internal concepts and replace them throughout the user-facing product.

1. Replace current algorithm structure

Replace the existing user-facing algorithm options:

Old:
- Modulo Rate
- AI Rate

New:
- Rules Rate
- Revenue Target AI Rate

Rules Rate should become the default option.

Revenue Target AI Rate should utilize the existing AI Rate formula and preserve the advanced logic that considers revenue growth targets, occupancy, vacancy, competitive rates, room attributes, leasing velocity, and guardrails.

2. Rules Rate logic

Rules Rate should calculate recommended rates using user-created stackable pricing rules.

Examples of stackable rules:
- Discount every room vacant more than 10 days by 5%
- Increase all units at campuses over 90% occupancy by 5%
- Add a premium for renovated units
- Cap rate increases at 3%
- Never price below the minimum approved rate
- Apply a move-in special to units vacant more than 30 days

Calculation flow:
1. Start with current rate or base market rate
2. Apply active stackable pricing rules
3. Apply guardrails / min-max bands
4. Produce final recommended rate

Explanation example:
Recommended rate: $4,750
Pricing Strategy: Rules Rate
Reason: This unit has been vacant for 12 days, so the 5% vacancy discount rule was applied. The final rate remains within approved guardrails.

3. Revenue Target AI Rate logic

Revenue Target AI Rate should replace the old user-facing “AI Rate” concept, but it should utilize the existing AI Rate formula as the foundation.

Do not rebuild the AI Rate formula from scratch unless absolutely necessary. Instead:
- Reuse the existing AI Rate calculation logic
- Rename it and present it as Revenue Target AI Rate
- Ensure the existing revenue target logic is included
- Preserve the existing factors, weights, adjustments, and guardrails where they are already working
- Clean up the explanation layer so users understand the recommendation without needing to understand the full formula

Revenue Target AI Rate should consider:
- Annual revenue growth target
- Actual YTD revenue growth
- Revenue gap to target
- Months remaining in the year
- Occupancy
- Days vacant
- Competitive rates
- Unit attributes
- Leasing velocity
- Room type performance
- Campus-level demand
- Guardrails / min-max bands

Calculation flow:
1. Start with the existing AI Rate formula
2. Include revenue growth target gap and urgency logic
3. Evaluate occupancy, vacancy, competitive rates, unit attributes, and leasing velocity
4. Apply the existing AI-driven adjustment recommendation
5. Apply guardrails / min-max bands
6. Produce final recommended rate

Explanation example:
Recommended rate: $4,950
Pricing Strategy: Revenue Target AI Rate
Reason: This campus is 1.8% behind its annual revenue growth target, occupancy is 92%, and this room type has leased quickly over the last 90 days. The final rate remains within approved guardrails.
