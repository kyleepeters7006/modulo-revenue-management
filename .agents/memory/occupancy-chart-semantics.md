---
name: Occupancy chart semantics
description: Visualization rule for occupancy comparisons versus additive portfolio distributions.
---

Occupancy percentages by service line, location, or room type must use a common
0–100% comparison scale, such as bars or dots. Do not put occupancy percentages
in a pie or donut chart.

**Why:** occupancy is a rate within each category, not an additive share of a
portfolio total. Dividing one occupancy percentage by the sum of all occupancy
percentages creates a number with no operational meaning.

**How to apply:** reserve pie/donut charts for additive measures such as units or
revenue. For occupancy, show each category's actual percentage directly.
